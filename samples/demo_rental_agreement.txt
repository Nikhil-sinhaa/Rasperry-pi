DEMO RENTAL AGREEMENT — FICTIONAL EXAMPLE ONLY

This fictional example is supplied only to test the Private Legal Document Triage Agent. It is not a real agreement and should not be signed or relied on.

1. PARTIES AND PREMISES
The owner, Cedar Lane Rentals (a fictional name), agrees to rent Apartment 12 to Aarav Example (a fictional name). The apartment address is intentionally omitted from this demo.

2. TERM
The rental period begins on 1 October 2026 and ends on 30 September 2027, unless ended earlier under a term stated in this document.

3. RENT
The tenant must pay rent of INR 20,000 per month on or before the fifth calendar day of each month. Payment must be made using the payment method agreed by the parties in writing.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The tenant must pay a security deposit of INR 40,000 before receiving the keys. The agreement says the deposit will be returned within 30 days after the tenant returns the keys, after deductions for unpaid amounts or damage beyond ordinary wear. The owner must provide an itemized list of deductions.

5. REPAIRS
The tenant must notify the owner in writing about a repair that affects the safe use of the apartment. The owner will arrange an inspection within seven calendar days after receiving the notice. This clause does not specify a deadline for completing every repair.

6. ENTRY
The owner will give at least 24 hours' notice before entering the apartment for a routine inspection. Notice is not required for an emergency that needs immediate attention.

7. LATE PAYMENT
If rent is late, the agreement says a late fee of 2% of the overdue rent applies for each month or part of a month that the amount remains unpaid. The agreement does not provide a worked example of how partial months are calculated.

8. SUBLETTING
The tenant must obtain the owner's written permission before subletting the apartment or allowing another person to take over the tenancy.

9. TENANT TERMINATION
After the first six months, the tenant may end the agreement by giving at least 30 days' written notice to the owner. The document does not specify whether notice must be delivered by email, post, or hand delivery.

10. OWNER TERMINATION FOR BREACH
If the tenant materially breaches this agreement, the owner must send a written notice describing the breach. The tenant has 15 days after receiving that notice to address the breach before the owner may issue a termination notice under this agreement.

11. CLEANING DEDUCTION
The owner may deduct a reasonable cleaning charge from the deposit if the apartment is not returned in a reasonable condition. The agreement does not define "reasonable cleaning charge" or list the cleaning tasks covered by that phrase.

12. CHANGES
Any change to this agreement must be written down and signed by both parties.
