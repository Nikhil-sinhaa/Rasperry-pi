### Interpretation guide

- **High `absent_unexpected_finding_pct`** → consider raising `MIN_RETRIEVAL_SIMILARITY` or investigate
  whether the model is hallucinating clause text. All unexpected findings must be reviewed by hand before
  submitting.
- **High `answerable_missed_pct`** → lower `MIN_RETRIEVAL_SIMILARITY` or review chunk size in `config.py`.
  May also indicate a mismatch between `QUESTIONS` phrasing in `cuad_to_gold.py` and retrieval queries.
- **Categories consistently 0/N** → check whether the clause wording is captured by any query in
  `TRIAGE_TOPIC_SEARCHES` (`legal_triage/retrieval.py`) and consider adding a targeted search query.
